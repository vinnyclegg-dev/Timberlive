# 01 — Opening build orders (Normal difficulty, cycles 1–3)

Numbers are from 02-numbers.md ([bp] unless noted). Community advice is cited [web] in sources.md. Easy: same order, with 40% consumption and 300 food / 250 water at start. Hard: 5–8 day temperates, so compress everything and get water storage before anything else.

## Starting position (all factions, Normal)
- 9 adults + 4 kits, 130 food, **0 water**. Kits work after 6 days.
- 13 beavers eat **34.7 food/day** and drink **27.6 water/day**, so the starting food lasts **≈3.7 days**. Carrots take 4 days and kohlrabi 3, so **food and water are urgent from minute one**.
- The District Center provides 4 builder/hauler slots. That leaves **5 adults** for workplaces until the kits grow up.
- **First drought** comes after 13–17 temperate days and lasts ≈2–3 days (5–9 × 0.38). The second is a bit longer. Full 5–9 day droughts arrive by cycle 6. **Badtides** are possible from **cycle 5**; the first one is short (≈1 day).
- Pause (Space) at load and lay out the whole first wave before unpausing.

## Universal day-0 checklist
1. Read the map: river, deepest water near the District Center, existing forest, berry bushes, flat irrigated (green) land, and the badwater source(s).
2. Paths to everything. Keep them short; beavers walk slowly. Keep everything within the district range (paths over ~70 tiles go red) [web].
3. Workplace priorities: water pump and farm highest, lumberjacks next, inventor lowest (so idle beavers become science).
4. Set lumberjack area: **cut live trees near the District Center**. Dead trees don't regrow; live ones leave stumps that Foresters replant.

## Folktails (FT)
**Day 0 (paused), in build order:**

| # | Build | Cost | Workers | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water Pump on the deepest water (≥1 m; reach 2 m) next to the DC | 12 Log | 1 | 0 starting water. One pump ≈ 48 water/day, enough for ~20 beavers |
| 2 | Gatherer Flag over existing berry bushes | free | 1 | Food now. Bushes give 0.25/day each after the initial crop, so it's a stopgap |
| 3 | 2 × Lumberjack Flag in live forest | free | 2 | Logs gate everything |
| 4 | Efficient Farmhouse + ~50 carrot tiles on green (irrigated) land | 25 Log | 3 (start with 1–2) | 13 × 2.67 / 0.75 ≈ 46 tiles. First harvest on day ~5 |
| 5 | 2–3 Small Warehouses (30 each) by the farm/DC + 1 Small Pile | 3 Log each | – | Food buffer |
| 6 | 3 × Small Tank (30 each) next to the pump | 15 Log each | – | Start banking water immediately |
| 7 | Lodges × 2–3 | 12 Log each | – | 3 beds each. Don't overbuild: free beds = births |
| 8 | Inventor | 12 Log | 1 (lowest priority) | 16 SP/day |
| 9 | Campfire | 15 Log | – | +1 wellbeing |

Worker budget: pump 1 + gatherer 1 + lumberjacks 2 + farmer 1 = 5. Add farmers and the inventor as kits grow up (day ~6). Pausing one lumberjack to staff the farm is fine [web].

**Days 1–6:**
- **Forester** (30 SP) first, then **Stairs** (70) if trees or water are on other levels.
- Plant a mixed forest: birch near (7 d / 1 log), pine (12 d / 2 logs + resin), oak far (30 d / 8 logs). Community figure for ~30 beavers: ~30 birch, 60 pine, 30 oak [web].
- **Lumber Mill** (15 Log, 0 SP) + **Power Wheel** (20 Log, 1 walker, 50 power) or a Water Wheel (50 Log, 270 power) on flowing water. Make only the planks you need; mills eat logs.
- Second food type for wellbeing: **potatoes → Grill** (25 Log, 0 SP; 1 log per 10 cycles), or sunflower (raw, 5 days).
- Keep ~5 tiles per beaver of carrots. Each FT farmhouse handles ~50 fast-crop tiles [web].

**Before the first drought (≈day 12–14):**
- Water banked ≥ **beavers × 2.12 × 4** (≈110–150): 4–5 Small Tanks *full*, or 1 Medium Tank (120 SP, 30 Plank 20 Gear, holds 300).
- Food banked ≥ beavers × 2.67 × (drought days + 4 days carrot regrow) ≈ 250. If the fields dry out you lose the crop in 2 days, then need 4 more to regrow. Fields beside the river or a dammed pool stay irrigated.
- Optional: a row of **Dams** (20 Log) across the river just downstream of the pump, if the river is narrow and close. It keeps a pool for the pump and the fields. Some players rush this dam instead of tanks [web].
- Population target **≈15–20** until water storage is solved [web consensus]. Don't build spare lodges.

**Cycle 2:**
- Science path: **Levee** (120) and **Platform** (100), then a 2-deep reservoir (deep beats wide).
  - **Medium Tank** (120) × 1–2.
  - **Floodgate** (150) to control the reservoir outlet.
  - **Gear Workshop** (100; needs 120 power).
  - **Medical Bed** (80).
- Second **Water Pump** at ~20 beavers. A second **Farmhouse** at 15–20 beavers (one set to plant, one to harvest).
- **Hauling Post** (20 Log 10 Plank, 0 SP, up to 10 haulers with ×2 carry) once hauling delays show. **Builders' Hut** (100 SP [bp]) for 4 more builders.
- Wellbeing basics: Shrubs (12 Log, 0 SP), Rooftop Terrace (15 Log), Teeth Grindstone (5 Log), Shower (50 SP).

**Cycle 3 (prepare for badtide, possible from cycle 5):**
- **Gristmill** (180) → **Bakery** (160) on wheat. 1.5 food per tile per day, the best FT density.
- **Double/Triple Lodge** (150/250) to free space.
- **Wind Turbine** (120) or more water wheels.
- **Discharge** (250) and a 3×3 irrigation pond.
- Floodgates on every reservoir inlet. Plan the badtide bypass.
- **Large Water Pump** (400) when population passes ~40.
- Work toward the **Contamination Sensor** (400) and **Weather Station** (300) for automated gates (see 04-automation.md). Herbalist (300) for antidotes.

## Iron Teeth (IT)
No reliable current IT walkthrough exists. This order is assembled from [bp] stats, the 1.0 notes, and an outdated 2021 guide [web].

**Key differences:**
- Growth is through **Breeding Pods** only (10 Log, 0 SP). Each kit costs **5 water + 5 berries over 5 days**. Pods breed regardless of housing.
- **Deep Water Pump** reaches 6 m. Farmhouses have 2 workers.
- Opening crop is **kohlrabi** (3 days, eaten raw, 0.67 food/tile/day).
- **Barrack** = 10 beds for 40 Log.
- Since 1.0 the Scavenger Flag (scrap metal) is available at start, and the **Metalsmith** (150 SP; scrap → Metal Parts) gates many IT buildings (Hydroponic Garden 10 MP, Steam Engine 40 MP, Numbercruncher 50 MP) [bp, web].

**Day 0 (paused):**

| # | Build | Cost | Workers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deep Water Pump on the deepest reachable water | 12 Log | 1 | ≈48 water/day; keeps working deep into droughts |
| 2 | Gatherer Flag on berries | free | 1 | Food *and* pod input. Protect the bushes |
| 3 | 2 × Lumberjack Flag | free | 2 | |
| 4 | Farmhouse + ~50 kohlrabi tiles | 20 Log | 2 | 13 × 2.67 / 0.67 ≈ 52 tiles; first harvest day ~3–4 |
| 5 | 3–4 Small Tanks, 2–3 Small Warehouses, 1 Small Industrial Pile | | | The 2021 guide needed 4–5 tanks for drought 1 (possibly outdated) |
| 6 | 1 Barrack | 40 Log | – | 10 beds is enough for a long time |
| 7 | Inventor (lowest priority) | 12 Log | 1 | |
| 8 | Campfire | 15 Log | | |

**Days 1–6:**
- Forester (30), Stairs (70).
- **1–2 Breeding Pods, switched on only while water and berries are in surplus.** A pod needs 1 water + 1 berry per day.
- Lumber: the **Industrial Lumber Mill** (20 Log, 2 workers, 75 power, 0 SP) with a **Large Power Wheel** (50 Log, 4 walkers, 300) or a Compact Water Wheel (25 Log, 120).
- Second crop: **cassava → Fermenter** (25 Log, 0 SP, 50 power, ×2.5 food).

**Before the first drought:**
- **Pause every Breeding Pod** (or automate: Weather Station Drought → NOT → pods).
- Water ≥ beavers × 2.12 × 4. Food ≥ beavers × 2.67 × (drought + 3 days kohlrabi regrow).
- The 2021 guide figure of ~180 food + 100 water for 13–15 beavers is consistent.

**Cycles 2–3:**
- Levee (120), Platform (100), Floodgate (150), Medium Tank (120).
- Scavenger Flag + **Metalsmith** (150).
- Gear Workshop (100).
- **Hydroponic Garden** (200; doesn't need irrigation, only water goods).
- Rowhouse (180) / Large Barrack (400).
- Corn → **Food Factory** (300).
- Badtide prep as for FT, but with **Irrigation Barrier** (400) and a **Decontamination Pod** (400).
- Later: Advanced Breeding Pod (1000; adults directly), **Numbercruncher** (1500; 10 SP/h, no workers).

## Opening mistakes to avoid
- Building homes before water and food. FT births follow free beds.
- Shallow pump intake. Once the river drops below the pipe, the pump stops; FT Water Pump reach is only 2 m.
- Cutting all nearby trees without a Forester. The wood crash comes in cycle 2.
- Running out of builders: 4 DC builders walking far is very slow. Add a Hauling Post and Builders' Hut, and tick "prioritize by haulers" on construction sites [web].
- An early second District Center. The wiki advises against it; use Hauling Posts and Builders' Huts instead [web].
- Treating berries as the main food. They're the worst food per tile; move to carrots or kohlrabi fast [web].
